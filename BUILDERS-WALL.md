# Builders' Wall - differentiation notes

Inspiration only (not a clone): [Gazi Jarin](https://www.gazijarin.com/) shows craft categories with limited previews and a personal art collection.

## What Gazi does
- Clear category headers (`/ software`, `/ hardware`, `/ art`)
- Few featured items + "View all" / explore collection
- **Her own** artwork gallery

## What this portfolio does differently (LinkedIn-safe originality)
Not another mini draw pad under the fold.

**Builders' Wall** - exhibition-first guestbook:
1. Visitors land on a **postcard masonry wall** (seed art so it never looks empty)
2. Each piece carries a **signal badge**: Hiring · Collaborating · Cheering · Curious
3. Filter chips by signal (useful for recruiters scanning the wall)
4. Drawing is **secondary** - "Open drawing studio" reveals a **large canvas**
5. Submissions are framed as **notes for builders**, not anonymous doodles

Why this is stronger for LinkedIn than copying Gazi:
- Collaborative + intentional (signal taxonomy) vs personal art showcase
- Recruiter-readable filters
- Studio / exhibition split (gallery default, canvas on demand)
- Distinct postcard visual language (warm paper cards on dark site)

Share angle: "I turned my portfolio into a builders' wall where visitors leave sketches tagged Hiring / Collaborating / Cheering / Curious."
